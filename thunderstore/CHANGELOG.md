# Changelog

## 1.4.0
- First public release.
- Belts, underground belts, station belts and splitters no longer cost gears.
- Machines now cost gears: 3 for level 1 (`_t1`), 5 for level 2 and everything else. Configurable per machine.
- Per-type toggles for belts, chests and pallets.
- Settings are localized (English, Spanish) and follow the game language.
